# Developer Requirements Report

## AI-Powered Gmail Campaign & Outreach SaaS

### 1. Executive Summary

Build a production-ready SaaS platform that allows users to:

* Connect their own Google/Gmail sender account through OAuth.
* Import prospect data from CSV, XLS, or XLSX.
* Create personalized email campaigns.
* Use Gemini AI to generate five campaign-specific email templates.
* Select and edit the preferred template.
* Personalize emails using prospect data.
* Schedule campaigns.
* Send through the user's authorized Gmail account.
* Control sending through a safety-first queue.
* Automatically stop or pause campaigns when provider or campaign risk signals occur.
* Track campaign activity and results.
* Maintain suppression/unsubscribe records.

The product should prioritize:

**Deliverability → Compliance → Reliability → User Experience → Scale**

It must never promise that every email will reach the inbox or that an email can never be classified as spam.

# 15. Safety-First Sending Engine

This is the most important backend component.

Before each email is processed, check:

```text
Campaign Active?
      ↓
Sender Connected?
      ↓
OAuth Valid?
      ↓
Recipient Valid?
      ↓
Recipient Suppressed?
      ↓
Campaign Limit Reached?
      ↓
Allowed Sending Window?
      ↓
Recent Provider Errors Normal?
      ↓
Safety Status OK?
      ↓
SEND
```

If a safety condition fails:

```text
DO NOT SEND
```

The job should be delayed, skipped, or the campaign paused depending on the condition.

---

# 16. Sending Strategy

Do **not** build the product around:

> "X emails per Gmail account is guaranteed safe."

Instead use:

### Application-level safety controls

* Configurable limits
* Gradual volume
* Controlled queue
* Sending windows
* Randomized scheduling within the permitted window
* Provider error detection
* Automatic backoff
* Automatic pause
* Campaign-level limits
* Sender-level limits

The system must respect the actual limits imposed by Google rather than attempting to work around them.

# 20. Deliverability Protection

The goal is:

**Reduce spam risk, not promise zero spam placement.**

The system should implement:

### Gradual Sending

Avoid sudden volume spikes.

### Controlled Scheduling

Spread jobs across the user's configured sending window.

### Recipient Quality Controls

Do not send to:

* Invalid addresses
* Suppressed addresses
* Previously hard-bounced addresses
* Unsubscribed contacts

### Content Quality

The AI generator should avoid:

* Misleading subjects
* False claims
* Excessive promotional language
* Fake personalization
* Unverifiable statements

### Automatic Pause

If abnormal failure/bounce signals appear:

```text
Campaign
   ↓
Risk Detected
   ↓
PAUSE
   ↓
Notify User
```

```text

```

Before every send:

```text
Recipient
   ↓
Suppression Check
   ↓
Suppressed?
 ┌───────┴───────┐
 YES             NO
 ↓                ↓
SKIP             CONTINUE
```

For applicable marketing traffic, the product should provide appropriate unsubscribe functionality and honor opt-out requests promptly.

# 37. Critical Product Rule

The product should **not market itself as**:

> "Send X emails every day without going to spam."

Instead:

> **"AI-powered Gmail campaigns with controlled scheduling, personalization, and deliverability-focused safeguards."**

The application should treat Gmail's own restrictions as authoritative. Google officially supports automated sending through the Gmail API, but API access does not mean Gmail guarantees inbox placement.

# 38. Final Recommended Product Positioning

### Product

**AI Gmail Campaign Manager**

### Core Value

> Connect your Google account, import your prospects, generate personalized campaign emails with AI, schedule them, and manage the entire campaign from one dashboard.

### Main Features

**Google OAuth**
→ Secure sender connection

**AI Templates**
→ 5 campaign-specific templates

**CSV/XLS Import**
→ Fast prospect onboarding

**Personalization**
→ Dynamic prospect fields

**Smart Scheduling**
→ Controlled sending windows

**Safety Engine**
→ Provider-aware rate controls and automatic pausing

**Suppression**
→ Prevent sending to blocked/unsubscribed contacts

**Analytics**
→ Campaign performance and activity

**Production Infrastructure**
→ Redis + BullMQ + Gmail API + MongoDB

### Most Important Engineering Principle

**Do not optimize the system for maximum email volume. Optimize it for reliable, compliant, controlled sending.**

That approach gives the SaaS a much stronger foundation for production, reduces the chance of users abusing the system, and avoids making a promise—"never spam"—that no software can technically guarantee.
